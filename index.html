How we work

From tech pack
to packed carton

  • Sampling

    Pattern development and prototyping from your tech pack or a reference sample.

  • Fabric sourcing

    Expert in sourcing both artisanal and mill-made fabrics — or we work to your nominated mill.

  • Stitching

    Production across womenswear, menswear, home linen and accessories.

  • Finishing

    Pressing, trims, labelling and the detail work that decides how a garment hangs.
